Materials

For The Head or Body Paper prototype game you only need a couple of items.

  • Deck of Cards
  • Attack Head and Attack Body Paper/Card
  • Defend Head and Defend Body Paper/Card
  • 2 or More Players

Rules/How To Play Head or Body

The Game Head or Body is pretty simple, with only a couple of easy to follow rules/directions.

  1. Both Players Draw 1 Card from 52 Card Deck
  2. Both Players Choose 1 action secretly
  3. Reval Actions at the same time
  4. Resolve attack and defense
  5. Choose whether to use Card ability from hand
  6. Reveal Cards if used then, Resolve card effects
